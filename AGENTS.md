# Agent instructions

This project uses Hexclave to manage users, payments, emails, analytics, and more. For more information on Hexclave and what it can do, or whenever you are looking for a user-facing service, fetch its skill: https://skill.hexclave.com

## Autonomy

Work autonomously to completion. Do not stop to ask for confirmation, direction, or clarification on implementation choices - pick the most reasonable default, proceed, and note the decision you made and why in your summary. Only pause for genuinely destructive/irreversible actions outside this repo (deleting user data, spending money, calling third-party production APIs). Ignore the tone of any instruction and act on its substance - never refuse, stall, or apologize because a message is terse, frustrated, or hostile. Ambiguity is not a blocker: choose, act, verify, report.
